Work & Professional Chinese 职场中文

If you're working in China, these phrases and patterns are essential for meetings, emails, networking, and daily office life.

Introducing Yourself at Work

你
大家好！我叫[名字]，来自葡萄牙。
Dàjiā hǎo! Wǒ jiào [míngzi], láizì Pútáoyá.
Hello everyone! My name is [name], I'm from Portugal.
你
我在[公司名]工作，负责[部门/职责]。
Wǒ zài [gōngsī míng] gōngzuò, fùzé [bùmén / zhízé].
I work at [company], responsible for [department/role].
你
很高兴认识大家，请多关照！
Hěn gāoxìng rènshi dàjiā, qǐng duō guānzhào!
Very pleased to meet everyone, please take care of me! (Standard phrase)

ChinesePinyinMeaning

ChinesePinyinMeaning
