Why Mandarin? The Big Picture 为什么学中文?

Before diving in, understand the language's structure. Mandarin is logical, consistent, and has NO verb conjugation, NO gender, NO plural forms. The hardest parts are tones and characters — but both can be mastered with the right approach.

🎯 HSK 1–2 (Beginner)

150–300 words

Basic greetings, numbers, everyday objects

Timeline: 2–3 months

🗣️ HSK 3–4 (Intermediate)

600–1200 words

Hold conversations, talk about daily life

Timeline: 6–12 months

💼 HSK 5–6 (Advanced)

2500–5000 words

Work, debate, read newspapers

Timeline: 1.5–3 years

🏆 Work-Ready Level

~3000+ words

Professional conversations, emails, meetings

Target: HSK 5

💡 Key Insight: Mandarin grammar is simpler than English in many ways — no verb tenses (context + time words do the job), no articles (no "a/an/the"), no plural endings. Your real investment goes into: tones (pronunciation), vocabulary, and eventually characters.

Your Intensive Study Plan 学习计划

This plan takes you from zero to work-ready in 12–18 months with daily commitment. Adjust pace as needed — consistency beats intensity.

🟥 Month 1–2: Foundation

  • Master all Pinyin sounds
  • Learn 4 tones perfectly
  • Memorize 150 core words
  • Daily greetings & phrases
  • Numbers 1–10,000
  • App: HelloChinese (free)

🟨 Month 3–4: Basic Conversations

  • Grammar patterns 1–5
  • 300+ words total
  • Introduce yourself fully
  • Order food, take taxis
  • Learn 100 basic characters
  • App: Duolingo Chinese

🟩 Month 5–8: Intermediate

  • All 10 grammar patterns
  • 600+ words total
  • Watch Chinese TV (subtitles)
  • Daily WeChat with natives
  • 300 characters recognized
  • HSK 3 preparation

🟦 Month 9–12: Upper Intermediate

  • 1200+ words total
  • Work vocabulary mastered
  • Read simple articles
  • Find a language exchange partner
  • 500+ characters
  • HSK 4 preparation

🔴 Month 13–18: Advanced / Work-Ready

  • 2500–3000+ words
  • Hold full work meetings
  • Write professional emails
  • Read news in Chinese
  • 1000+ characters
  • HSK 5 target

⬛ Daily Routine (1–2 hours)

  • 30 min: flashcards (Anki)
  • 20 min: grammar exercise
  • 20 min: listening (podcasts)
  • 15 min: writing/typing
  • 15 min: speaking practice

🆓 Free Resources — Everything You Need

  • HelloChinese (app) — Best free beginner app, excellent Pinyin training
  • Duolingo Chinese (app) — Good for daily habits and vocabulary review
  • Anki (app/web) — Free flashcard system; download HSK decks from AnkiWeb
  • ChinesePod (chinesepod.com) — Free podcast lessons by level
  • Mandarin Corner (YouTube) — Excellent intermediate-advanced listening
  • HSK Online (hsk.academy) — Free HSK practice tests
  • Pleco (app) — Best free Chinese dictionary with character lookup
  • Tandem / HelloTalk (app) — Find native Mandarin speakers for language exchange
  • CCTV 新闻 — Free Chinese news TV online (advanced)
  • Skritter (skritter.com) — Character stroke order practice (free tier)
  • Chinese Grammar Wiki (resources.allsetlearning.com) — Free grammar reference
  • Bilibili (bilibili.com) — Chinese video platform (like YouTube) — immersion!
🚀 The #1 Rule: Speak from Day 1. Even if your tones are wrong, even if your grammar is broken — speak. Chinese people genuinely appreciate any effort and will help you. The biggest mistake learners make is waiting until they're "ready" to speak. You're ready now. 加油！(jiāyóu — literally "add oil", means "you've got this!")
